# JSS submission copy: transfer from JISA

**Manuscript:** "It Is Not the Signature: An Empirical Study of a Hidden
Key-Parse Cost in JSON Web Token Validation" (36 pages, Elsevier preprint
format, line numbers). Article type: **Research paper**.
**Route:** Elsevier Article Transfer Service (JISA → Journal of Systems and
Software), started from the transfer offer link in Elsevier's e-mail.

## Steps

1. Open the transfer offer link while signed in to your Elsevier account
   and choose **Journal of Systems and Software**. Accept the transfer.
2. Elsevier creates a draft submission in JSS's Editorial Manager with your
   old JISA files. Log in to JSS's Editorial Manager and open it (usually
   under "Submissions Sent Back to Author" or "Incomplete Submissions").
3. **Remove every old JISA file** (manuscript, cover letter, highlights,
   figures, anything else) and upload the files below.
4. Check title, abstract, keywords, all six authors with affiliations and
   ORCID, and the corresponding author (Dr. Tze Hui Liew).
5. Build the PDF, check it is the new 36-page version, and press **Submit**.
   Accepting the offer alone does not submit the paper.

Do not also submit the paper to JSS as a new submission: that would be a
duplicate.
